Marginal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Marginal is an iOS game that teaches microeconomics. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. It applies to the Marginal app (the App Store listing "Marginal - Play Economics").

What we collect

  • Account information. You can play as a guest, which creates an anonymous account with a random ID. You can save your account with Sign in with Apple or with an email code. Then we receive your account ID and the email address you provide (with Apple, this may be Apple's private relay address). Teachers need a saved account.
  • Nickname. A display name you choose, shown to the other members of classes or groups you join.
  • Game progress. Your saved game and progress (businesses, scores, lessons completed), so you can restore it on a new phone. You have your own private company. You also get a separate company in each class or group you join, and that company is shared with the class or group.
  • Classes and groups. The classes or groups you create or join with a code, and your membership in them. Members of a class or group see each other's nicknames, scoreboard runs and businesses in that class or group's company. A teacher can also see the progress, quiz results and net worth history of a student's company in the teacher's class. A teacher cannot see your own private company or a friend group's companies. A teacher can type a real name next to a student's nickname in a private roster that only that teacher can read.
  • Feedback you send. If you use Send Feedback, we store your message and category, and optionally the app version, device model and some game details.
  • Usage events. The app sends a short code when you reach a few steps: you see the intro, place your first machine, finish your first game day, find a lesson, come back 1, 7 or 30 days after you started, or join a class or group. Each is stored with the app version, the platform, the time and your random account ID. They hold no name and no typed text.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes or freezes, iOS gives the app a report. The app sends a short summary of it: the type of error and which parts of the app were running. It holds no name and no typed text.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, location, contacts, photos or payment information. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

How we use it

Only to run the game: syncing your progress, showing leaderboards and classes, and reading your feedback to improve the game. We also count usage events and read crash reports to see where new players get stuck and to fix crashes.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and protected by access rules so players can only change their own data. Game progress is also saved on your device.

Our website is hosted by Cloudflare. When the app makes a guest account or sends you an email code, it runs a bot check from Cloudflare (Turnstile). Cloudflare sees your device's network address and browser details for that check. If you ask for an email sign-in code, Cloudflare's email service delivers the message, so it handles your email address and the code. A feedback message is read by an automated assistant that sorts reports into bug reports. It sees the message, category, screen, app version and device, but not your account ID. Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees your network address when you open a page. Marginal opens Wikipedia articles in an in-app browser for further reading; Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies there.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Marginal sends Apple your scores and achievements: your career score, net worth, days to reach $10 million, days to win each challenge, and the achievements you earn. It sends them about once for each game day. Game Center shows them on its leaderboards under your Game Center name, and Apple's privacy policy applies. Who can see them depends on your Game Center settings. Marginal does not send your Marginal nickname or account ID to Game Center, and our servers receive nothing from it. If you are not signed in to Game Center, nothing is sent.

Features that stay on your device

  • Apple Intelligence. On devices that have it, some features explain things in the game, such as an advisor, the news and consultants. Apple's on-device model writes the text. Your question and the answer stay on your device. Marginal does not send them anywhere.
  • AI help in classes. A teacher chooses whether features that could help with quiz answers are on for their class. They are off until the teacher turns them on. We store that one setting with the class. Nothing you type into these features is stored or sent.
  • The widget. The home screen widget shows your company name, the day, profit, net worth and when the board meets. The game saves these to a file that only the game and its widget can read, on your device.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight. Lessons, glossary terms and challenges can be found and opened by name. The list is built on your device.
  • The browser preview. The early preview at marginal.yipbit.com/play/ keeps your game in your browser's own storage. It makes no account and does not send your game to our servers.

How long we keep it

  • Guest accounts. If you play as a guest and never save anything online, we remove the account once it is more than 90 days old, counted from the day the account was created, not your last visit. An account that has scores, progress, a saved game, a class or group it joined or created, a business or offer in a class town, a roster name, or feedback is not removed this way.
  • Classes and groups you delete. A class or group has to be archived before it can be deleted. When its owner deletes it, it is removed at once, with the progress, businesses, runs, net worth history and offers its players published there, and any roster names the teacher added. Each player keeps their own copy of their company on their phone and in their private cloud save.
  • Archived classes and groups. Archiving stops new joins and stops players publishing new progress there. An archived class or group is kept until its owner deletes it. One that has been archived for more than a year is removed automatically, on the first day of each month.
  • Usage events and crash reports. They have no expiry date yet. When you delete your account they stay, without the link to you.

Apple Schoolwork

Some schools use Apple School Manager and Apple's Schoolwork app. This only applies to a device signed in with a school-managed Apple Account. On that device, when a student does a Marginal lesson or challenge, Marginal passes progress to Apple's ClassKit: how long the student spent, the quiz score on a lesson (the questions answered right on the first try, out of the questions tried), and for a challenge, whether it was won or how far the student got. Marginal also lists its lessons and challenges with ClassKit so teachers can assign them. Teachers see the progress in Schoolwork for the activities they assign. Apple handles it under the school's agreement with Apple. Marginal's own servers do not receive or store any of it. On any other device, nothing is passed to ClassKit. A school manages Schoolwork in Apple School Manager and in the Schoolwork app, and a parent can ask the school what it has turned on.

Children

Marginal is for players 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it. Schools use Marginal in classrooms. We ask only for a nickname and do not ask for personal information such as real names. Please choose a nickname that is not your real name. Teachers and schools can read more at Marginal for schools.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your online scores, progress and class memberships. You can also email us and we will delete your data.
