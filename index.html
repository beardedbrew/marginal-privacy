Marginal Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Marginal is an iOS game that teaches microeconomics. This policy explains what information the game collects and how it is used. It applies to the Marginal app (the App Store listing "Marginally - Play Economics").

What we collect

  • Account information. You can play as a guest, which creates an anonymous account with a random ID. If you save your account with Sign in with Apple, Google or an email address, we receive your account ID and the email address you provide (with Apple, this may be Apple's private relay address).
  • Nickname. A display name you choose, shown on leaderboards and in classes or groups you join.
  • Game progress. Your saved game (businesses, scores, lessons completed) so it can sync and appear on leaderboards.
  • Classes and groups. The classes or groups you create or join with a code, and your membership in them. A teacher can see the nicknames and scores of students in their class.
  • Feedback you send. If you use Send Feedback, we store your message and category, and optionally the app version, device model and some game details.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, location, contacts, photos or payment information. We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

How we use it

Only to run the game: syncing your progress, showing leaderboards and classes, and reading your feedback to improve the game.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, and protected by access rules so players can only change their own data. Game progress is also saved on your device. Marginal opens Wikipedia articles in an in-app browser for further reading; Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies there.

Apple Schoolwork

Some schools use Apple School Manager and Apple's Schoolwork app. This only applies to a device signed in with a school-managed Apple Account. On that device, when a student does a Marginal lesson or challenge, Marginal passes progress to Apple's ClassKit: how long the student spent, the quiz score on a lesson (the questions answered right on the first try, out of the questions tried), and for a challenge, whether it was won or how far the student got. Marginal also lists its lessons and challenges with ClassKit so teachers can assign them. Teachers see the progress in Schoolwork for the activities they assign. Apple handles it under the school's agreement with Apple. Marginal's own servers do not receive or store any of it. On any other device, nothing is passed to ClassKit. A school manages Schoolwork in Apple School Manager and in the Schoolwork app, and a parent can ask the school what it has turned on.

Children

Marginal is designed to be used in classrooms. We ask only for a nickname and do not ask for personal information such as real names. Please choose a nickname that is not your real name.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. This permanently deletes your online scores, progress and class memberships. You can also email us and we will delete your data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
